Fund a music therapy room that carries a donor's name

A tangible, well-documented project, with a finished reference already in use.

Request a one-page project briefDownload the case study (PDF)

Most of the new music rooms in Israeli rehabilitation centers and hospitals were funded by donors. In the sample project, the hospital contracted the work and a dedicated gift funded it; the studio now bears the name of Valerie and Michael Miller. I work with the clinical team and the development office, so you have something concrete to show a donor before work begins.

How it works:

  1. 1

    The clinical team defines the need: who will use the room, for which groups, and in which space. The needs assessment is free of charge, and it usually ends with an initial room program.

  2. 2

    I prepare a one-page project brief for the donor: renderings, scope and timeline, coordinated with the team. The cost estimate follows a site survey and goes directly to the institution.

  3. 3

    The development office presents it to the donor. Once approved, I build and document every stage, so there is progress to share along the way and at the dedication.

About dedications:

Naming and dedication options are set by the institution and its policies. My part is to deliver a room that is easy to present, photograph and dedicate.
