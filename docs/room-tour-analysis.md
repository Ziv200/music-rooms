## Structured inventory (order of narration)

1. **Smart lighting remote** — wall-mounted magnetic/detachable remote by the entrance for lighting scenes.
2. **Acoustic treatment** — high-end panels on walls and ceiling; bass traps in corners.
3. **Guitar station** — multi-guitar rack: acoustic, electric, two classical guitars.
4. **Guitar amplifier** — next to the guitars.
5. **Keyboard station** — Kawai keyboard with matching chair.
6. **Electronic drum kit** — EFNOTE; chosen for lower noise near the hospital entrance.
7. **Hand drum** — Roland HandSonic electronic hand percussion.
8. **Bass station** — bass guitar with bass amp / related low-end reinforcement.
9. **Piano** — Yamaha acoustic upright with bench.
10. **Microphones** — two vocal mics on floor stands.
11. **Acoustic curtains** — heavy curtains for absorption and to limit spill to the corridor.
12. **Equipment rack** — communications / sound cabinet (interfaces, processors).
13. **Main control desk** — studio desk with PC, second HandSonic, keyboard/mouse.
14. **Atmos listening station** — central armchair, 12-speaker immersive array.
15. **iPad control** — desk iPad with custom room UI (macros + digital mixer).

## Lighting & iPad control

- Entrance magnetic smart switch: on/off and lighting presets (e.g. quieter listening look with spotlight on the Atmos chair; brighter play/therapy look).
- Desk iPad: global room macros (including an Atmos button that shifts lighting) and a digital mixer with faders for mics, guitars, piano, electronic drums, and Bluetooth.

## Atmos listening position

- Described as the crown jewel of the work, optional upgrade in sales positioning.
- **12 speakers:** 4 ceiling + 7 floor-level ring + 1 subwoofer around a central armchair.
- Sitting experience described as striking (“wow”).
